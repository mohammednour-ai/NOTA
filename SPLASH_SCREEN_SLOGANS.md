## 💡 Typography Tips for Slogan

### Recommended Styling:
- **Font Weight:** 300-400 (light to regular, not bold)
- **Font Size:** 1.4-1.6rem (smaller than main headline)
- **Letter Spacing:** 0.05-0.1em (slightly increased)
- **Text Transform:** Sentence case or Title Case
- **Opacity:** 90-95% (slightly softer than headline)
- **Position:** Between logo and main headline
