## 💎 Design Principles (Amazon Design System + Meta AI Standards)

### 1. Clarity Over Cleverness
- Logo must be instantly recognizable at 16x16px
- Works in grayscale/single color
- No unnecessary details

### 2. Scalability First
- Vector-based design
- Maintains legibility 10px to 10ft
- No fine lines that disappear when small

### 3. Memorable & Unique
- Distinctive from competitors (Sephora, Fragrantica, FragranceX)
- Ownable visual equity
- Timeless, not trendy

### 4. AI-Enhanced Luxury
- Suggests intelligence without being techy/cold
- Maintains warmth and sensory appeal
- Premium materials/finishes implied

### 5. Inclusive & Unbiased
- Gender-neutral core design
- Adaptable color system (pink/blue/purple)
- Accessible to all cultures

## 🎬 Prompt Engineering Strategy

### Structure Formula
```
[Core Subject] + [Style Descriptors] + [Color Palette] + 
[Technical Specs] + [Mood/Emotion] + [Negative Prompts]
```

### Power Words That Work
- **Luxury:** Premium, sophisticated, elegant, refined, timeless
- **Tech:** AI-powered, intelligent, precision, innovative, cutting-edge
- **Visual:** Minimalist, clean, negative space, vector, scalable
- **Quality:** Ultra HD, studio lighting, professional, award-winning

### Avoid These Words
- ❌ "Cute" (too informal)
- ❌ "Fun" (undermines luxury)
- ❌ "Sparkly" (suggests cheap)
- ❌ "Modern" alone (overused, vague)
