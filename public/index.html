Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the recommendations?

Our AI has a 98% satisfaction rate. We analyze your complete preference profile, not just a single factor, ensuring highly personalized matches.

Is it really free?

Yes! NOTA is completely free. We earn a small commission when you purchase through our affiliate links, but it doesn't cost you extra.
